## 1. What Botditor does

Botditor scores new comments in a subreddit with AI (Google Gemini) and rule-based checks. Depending on the settings its moderators choose, it can:

- remove comments,
- report them for review,
- send warning messages to their authors.

It also gives moderators tools to bulk-remove comment threads, ban users, and manage an allow-list. The [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) explains what data this involves.

## 3. Automated moderation has limits

Botditor's scores come from automated systems and **can be wrong**. It may remove or report acceptable comments and miss harmful ones.

We recommend that you:

- start in dry-run mode,
- tune the thresholds for your community,
- keep human review in the loop.

Botditor never replaces your judgment as a moderator.
